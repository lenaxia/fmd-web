    Just a head-up

    We will handle all reports and corrections over the weekend. We are currently focusing on uploading new content to the site. By our estimate, we are more than 10,000 books behind, so please bear with us. We understand that some of you feel a complete overhaul of the site wasn’t necessary, so we’d like to explain why we did it. The old website was built in 2015, and many of the libraries it relied on have since been abandoned or are no longer maintained by their authors. This left the site with security vulnerabilities that could have been exploited at any time, and it was becoming increasingly expensive to maintain. The overhaul was long overdue, and the new site gives us a safer, more sustainable foundation going forward. Thank you for your patience and understanding
